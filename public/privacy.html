Privacy policy.

What happens to information you share with SAIXORA online.

Last updated: 21 September 2026 · Applies to the SAIXORA website

ScopeInformationPurposesContact formCookiesSharingSecurityRetention & rightsSchool dataContact
This notice covers the public website and its restricted installer downloads. School applications require a separate school-specific privacy notice and agreement.

Who this notice covers

This notice describes information handled when you visit the SAIXORA Technology website, submit a project enquiry, or use its restricted Secure Nova installer download area. SAIXORA is a student-founded technology initiative; the legal contracting party for paid projects will be identified in the relevant written agreement.

It does not describe personal data processed inside a school’s deployed Secure Nova applications. Before any school deployment, the school and service provider must establish the applicable privacy notice, monitoring permissions, data roles and service agreement.

Information we receive

If you submit a project enquiry, the form asks for your name, email address, subject and message. It also asks you to tick a consent checkbox to authorize using those details to reply. The checkbox is validated in your browser; the current form script does not send its value to the external form endpoint.

When you use the restricted download page, your submitted PIN is sent to the SAIXORA server for verification. The server uses your connection address in temporary memory to limit repeated failed attempts. Successful access creates a short-lived session cookie; the current download server does not create a named school account.

Like other websites, our hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser information and error logs to deliver and protect the site. Actual host logging and retention depend on the hosting provider selected.

Why we use information

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential project or demonstration.
  • Verify access to restricted installer files and help protect the download area against repeated incorrect PIN attempts.
  • Operate, troubleshoot and secure the website and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Contact form and delivery status

The contact form requires a separately configured delivery endpoint before it can send any enquiry. In the current supplied website package, that endpoint is blank and the form displays a configuration message instead of sending your message. We must update this notice to name or describe the actual email/form provider when one is connected.

If you contact us by another channel, that channel’s service provider may process your message under its own terms and privacy information.

Cookies and external resources

The download area issues an HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict access cookie after successful PIN verification. It is intended to expire after approximately 15 minutes and is used to recognize your authorized download session, not for advertising.

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these files, Google may receive technical connection information. We have not verified whether the selected production host adds analytics, advertising cookies or other tracking; this notice must be revised before any such tools are enabled.

Who may receive information

People authorized to handle SAIXORA enquiries may view the messages you send. The website host and any subsequently configured form or email delivery provider may process information needed to supply those services. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or to address misuse or security incidents.

We do not use enquiry details to sell a contact list. We do not present the school download PIN as a substitute for application-level authentication.

Security and your choices

The download server uses a short-lived signed session cookie, server-side PIN checking and limits repeated failed attempts. The server is intended to be hosted behind HTTPS before public launch. These measures do not make a shared PIN equivalent to individual school accounts or guarantee complete security.

Please do not send passwords, student lists, classroom recordings, medical details or other sensitive information through the general project enquiry form. Contact us through the contact page or another confirmed business channel to ask about the information you provided.

Retention, correction and deletion

The current server holds failed-attempt information temporarily in memory for rate limiting, and the browser access cookie expires after approximately 15 minutes. Enquiries, once a delivery provider is configured, may be retained in that provider and in our inbox until no longer reasonably needed for the enquiry, agreed work or applicable obligations. A specific production retention period has not yet been established.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted by contacting us. We will review the request against applicable legal requirements and legitimate record-keeping needs. Do not assume that deleting a browser cookie removes a message already submitted to a separate provider.

Schools and younger visitors

This general website is intended for business and school enquiries, not for collecting students’ personal information. An authorized adult should handle enquiries or software arrangements involving a school. Do not send information about children through the public contact form.

If Secure Nova is deployed, its separate privacy and monitoring documents must explain the school’s purposes, access permissions, retention and how affected individuals are informed.

Contact and updates

For privacy requests or questions, use the Contact page once its delivery service is operational, or a verified contact channel already provided to you by SAIXORA. We will publish a verified responsible contact and legal business identity before accepting live production enquiries or paid orders.

We may revise this notice to reflect changes in website services, vendors or law, and will update the date displayed above. This page describes the website’s current supplied implementation; it is not a certification of regulatory compliance.
